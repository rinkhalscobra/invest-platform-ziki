Secure sign-in. Sign in with your account. Keep your password private and enable two-factor authentication when available.

Dashboard overview. Start here: review portfolio value, available balance, active positions, performance, and market cards before placing any trade.

Check existing exposure. Review open positions and recent activity first. This prevents accidental overexposure or duplicating an existing position.

Open Futures. Choose Futures from the main navigation. Futures use leverage, so both gains and losses can grow quickly.

Choose the crypto pair. Use the pair selector to choose the market. Confirm the symbol and live price before entering an order.

Market or limit order. Market executes near the current price. Limit waits for your chosen price. Always verify the order type before continuing.

Set leverage and margin mode. Lower leverage gives more room before liquidation. Isolated limits risk to this position; cross can use more of the account balance.

Enter position size. Enter the asset amount and review required margin, spread cost, and liquidation price. Position size should come from your risk limit—not from the maximum available balance.

Add protection. Set stop loss before entry to cap planned risk, and take profit to define the exit. A long benefits if price rises; a short benefits if price falls.

Final review—do not rush. Before pressing Open Long or Open Short, re-check pair, side, size, leverage, margin mode, fees, stop loss, and take profit. This walkthrough does not submit an order.

Open CFD trading. CFDs let you speculate on instruments such as forex, commodities, indices, or stocks without owning the underlying asset. They are leveraged and high risk.

CFD size is measured in lots. Confirm the instrument and contract size. Enter lots—not dollars. Even 0.01 lot can represent meaningful market exposure.

Review CFD costs and risk. Check spread, required margin, leverage, swap or overnight costs, and liquidation risk. Market hours and liquidity differ by instrument.

Long versus short. Use Long only when your plan expects a rise; use Short only when it expects a fall. Add stop loss and take profit, then verify every field before submission.

Order and position management. After submission, monitor Positions, Open Orders, and Position History. Cancel unwanted pending orders and close positions deliberately—never assume a browser close exits a trade.

Safe trading checklist. Use small risk, understand leverage, confirm every order, and never trade money you cannot afford to lose. This video is a platform guide—not financial advice.
